Terms of Service

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Overview of Services

Revise ("we", "us", or "our") provides reverse logistics, inspection, refurbishment, and recommerce services for business partners ("Partners"). We act as a consignment partner, taking possession of returned or open-box inventory to resell it on secondary markets.

2. The Process & Routing

Partners are responsible for updating their return shipping addresses to route returned packages directly to the Revise facility. Revise does not bear the cost of inbound shipping unless explicitly agreed upon in a separate contract.

3. Inspection & Cosmetic Cleaning Only

Upon receiving a package, Revise conducts a documented inbound inspection. Cosmetic Cleaning Only: Our services are strictly limited to visual detailing, superficial cleaning, and repackaging. We DO NOT perform mechanical, electronic, or structural repairs. Items requiring technical repairs to function will be classified as unprocessable and handled according to our return or recycling protocol. Damage in Transit: Revise is not liable for items damaged prior to arriving at our facility. Fraud Clause: If a package contains counterfeit goods or missing essential components, it will be quarantined and the Partner notified immediately.

4. Pricing & Mutual Agreement

The listing price on secondary markets is determined through mutual consultation and agreement between Revise and the Partner. While we provide market insights and pricing recommendations, both parties must agree on the initial listing price and any subsequent price reductions (floor pricing).

5. Revenue Share & Secondary Returns

Revise retains a predetermined commission percentage from the final sale price. Payouts are made monthly. Secondary Returns: If a buyer on a secondary market returns the item to Revise (in accordance with platform rules), no payout will be issued to the Partner for that specific item until it is successfully and permanently resold.

6. Unsold Inventory (30-Day Policy)

Items are initially listed on secondary markets for a maximum period of 3 to 4 weeks. If an item remains unsold, we will consult with the Partner to mutually agree on either a price reduction or the return of the item. If a price reduction is authorized, the item will enter an additional 1-week clearance period. If it remains unsold after this final week, the item will be returned to the Partner at their expense, or recycled upon mutual agreement.

7. Facility Liability

If an item is irreparably damaged, destroyed, or lost while physically in Revise's sole possession due to our direct negligence, Revise will reimburse the Partner based on the mutually agreed estimated secondary market value of the item, not the original retail price (MSRP).

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Role as a Data Processor (B2B)

When processing returned packages, Revise acts solely as a Data Processor on behalf of the Partner (the Data Controller). We may temporarily encounter original customer shipping labels on incoming packages.

Strict Destruction Policy: We only use the original customer data (name, address, tracking number) to verify the return against the Partner's manifest. Immediately following verification, all original shipping labels containing personally identifiable information (PII) are physically destroyed and discarded. We do not store, harvest, or market to the Partner's original customers.

2. Data Collection (End-Buyers)

When Revise successfully resells an item on a secondary market (e.g., Vinted, Hardverapró, eBay), we act as the Data Controller for the new buyer. We collect the buyer's name, shipping address, and contact details strictly for fulfillment, shipping, and tax invoicing purposes. This data is retained only as long as required by national tax and accounting laws.

3. Business Partner Data & Deletion Rights (GDPR)

We collect business contact information (Name, Company Email, Website URL) submitted through our website strictly to provide ROI calculations and business proposals. We do not sell this B2B data to third parties. You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your data at any time by contacting us via email.
